About

I am an incoming Electrical and Computer Engineering PhD student at Johns Hopkins University, where I'm advised by Prof. Laixi Shi. I completed my undergraduate degree in Automation at Zhejiang University in 2025. During my undergraduate years, I was a visiting student researcher at Berkeley AI Research (BAIR), UC Berkeley, working with Prof. Gopala Anumanchipalli.

Email: xuanruzhou15 at gmail.com | xzhou155 at jhu.edu

Google Scholar / Github / LinkedIn

Research Overview

I'm interested in the following areas from different perspectives:

  • Machine intelligence
    • Multimodal perception and reasoning
    • Agentic RL - long-horizon reasoning, planning, tool use, and multi-agent orchestration
    • World models, autonomous decision-making
    • Causality, first-principles thinking
    • Neuro-symbolic AI
    • Alignment and universal representation learning
  • Computational cognitive science
    • Human-like concept learning and abstract reasoning
    • Theory of mind and social cognition in agents
    • Sample-efficient learning, compositional generalization
